Construction

Every icon is drawn on the same grid with the same pen, so a new one always looks like it belongs.

Drawing rules
Grid24 × 24, with 2 clear on every side
Stroke1.5, thinning to 1.25 at 20 and 1 at 16
EndsRound caps and round joins
CornersRadius 2 on boxes, matching the cards
FillOutline only. Small dots are the one solid shape
BalanceCircles reach the keyline, squares stop just inside it
MeaningOne icon per idea, never reused for something else
